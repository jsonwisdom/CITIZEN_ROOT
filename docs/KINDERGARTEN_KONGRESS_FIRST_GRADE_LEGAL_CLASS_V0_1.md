## Offices and Jobs

### 1. Sidecar Susie — *Protect the speaker*

**Question:** Did they get to finish?

### 2. Clarity Clyde — *Interrogate the rule*

**Question:** What rule is this? Who made it?

### 3. Justice Judy — *Check the evidence*

**Question:** Seen, guessed, or missing?

### 4. Mental Michael — *Audit the thinker*

**Question:** How do you know that?

---

## Claim Statuses

- **I SAW** — observation
- **I THINK** — interpretation
- **I FEEL** — experience
- **I KNOW** — supported
- **I DON'T KNOW** — valid
- **I CHANGED MY MIND** — learning

---

## Session Order

1. **Claim** — one speaker, one sentence if possible
2. **Susie** — speaker finishes
3. **Clyde** — name the rule, or say there isn’t one
4. **Judy** — split seen / guessed / missing
5. **Michael** — ask the speaker, not the room
6. **Status** — speaker marks one; room may mark “I don’t know yet”
7. **Close** — no winner, no required vote, no new authority
