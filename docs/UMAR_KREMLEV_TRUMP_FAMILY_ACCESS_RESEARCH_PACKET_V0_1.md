## Connection map

### Night Wolves

Russian investigative outlet Proekt reports that Kremlev joined the Night Wolves motorcycle club in the early 2010s. The club has longstanding public ties to Vladimir Putin and the Russian state. Proekt reports that Night Wolves leader Alexander Zaldostanov introduced Kremlev to Alexei Rubezhnoi.

Kremlev later said biker culture was not close to him.

### Putin security apparatus

Alexei Rubezhnoi is a senior official in Russia's Federal Protective Service and leads the Presidential Security Service responsible for protecting Putin.

Proekt reports:

- Rubezhnoi became Kremlev's patron.
- Rubezhnoi helped install Kremlev in leadership of the Russian Boxing Federation in 2017.
- Kremlev is related to Rubezhnoi through marriage.

These are attributed investigative findings, not independently adjudicated findings in this packet.

### Russian boxing and state-company network

Kremlev became president of the International Boxing Association in 2020. Under his leadership, Gazprom became its major financial backer.

The International Olympic Committee withdrew recognition from the IBA in 2023 following longstanding governance, finance, integrity and refereeing concerns.

The Russian Boxing Federation's supervisory structure also included Rubezhnoi and Rosneft chief Igor Sechin, a longstanding Putin ally.

### Betting and lottery interests

Proekt reports that Kremlev's network became a dominant beneficiary of state restructuring in Russia's betting market, with interests spanning major bookmakers, betting-payment infrastructure and regulation.

This edge remains classified as an attributed investigative finding pending object-by-object corporate and statutory replay.

### Rolf automobile group

Reuters confirmed this sequence:

1. Putin placed Rolf, Russia's largest car dealership, under temporary state management in December 2023.
2. A Russian court transferred the founder's stake and income to the state in February 2024, effectively nationalizing it.
3. Rolf announced Kremlev as the new owner of its holding company in September 2024.
4. Acquisition terms were not disclosed.

Rolf reported 323.8 billion rubles (approximately $3.63 billion) in 2023 revenue.

### Putin relationship

The public record includes Kremlev appearing with Putin, traveling in Putin-associated delegations, publicly praising Putin and receiving the Order of Friendship in April 2026.

These facts establish high-level access and state recognition. They do not, standing alone, prove that any wedding payment was directed by Putin or the Russian state.

### Donald Trump Jr.

- Kremlev and Donald Trump Jr. appeared together at an IBA forum in Istanbul in September 2025.
- Don Jr.'s representative describes Kremlev as a personal friend.
- Kremlev funded major portions of the May 2026 wedding celebrations.
- Kremlev and the couple publicly acknowledge the friendship and gift.
