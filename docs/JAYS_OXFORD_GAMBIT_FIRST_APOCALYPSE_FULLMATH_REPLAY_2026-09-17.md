## FullMATH audit

### A. Who identified the Greek fragments?

**Receipt:** University of Texas at Austin, 2017 announcement.

UT Austin identified Geoffrey Smith and Brent Landau as UT Austin religious-studies scholars who discovered the Greek fragments at Oxford University.

Disposition:

```text
DISCOVERY_AT_OXFORD_UNIVERSITY = PROVEN
DISCOVERERS_WERE_UT_AUSTIN_SCHOLARS = PROVEN
"OXFORD RESEARCHERS" IF IT MEANS OXFORD EMPLOYEES = NOT SUPPORTED
LOCATION != AFFILIATION
```

This is an Oxford Gambit meaning-delta example: "researchers at Oxford" can correctly describe location while incorrectly implying institutional affiliation.

### B. What is the manuscript?

Oxford's Oxyrhynchus Papyri project identifies P.Oxy. 5533 as Greek fragments of the First Apocalypse of James, formally published in volume LXXXVI in 2021.

A later comparative study describes the surviving Greek leaves as a fourth- or fifth-century manuscript. The 2017 UT announcement described them as fifth- or sixth-century fragments.

Disposition:

```text
P_OXY_5533_IDENTIFICATION = PROVEN
FORMAL_PUBLICATION_2021 = PROVEN
MANUSCRIPT_DATE = RANGE / SCHOLARLY_REFINEMENT
2017_PRESS_RANGE != FINAL_IMMUTABLE_DATE
```

### C. Teacher / classroom-copy claim

The 2017 announcement says the manuscript was *probably* a teacher's model because of neat handwriting and extensive syllable division by mid-dots. Later scholarship continues to discuss an educational context.

Disposition:

```text
EDUCATIONAL_CONTEXT = SCHOLARLY_INFERENCE
TEACHERS_MODEL = PROVEN_AS_REPORTED_HYPOTHESIS
TEACHERS_MODEL = NOT DIRECTLY_OBSERVED_FACT
```

The word "appears" in the screenshot is materially important and should not be removed.

### D. Toll collectors and scripted answers

The Coptic Nag Hammadi witness of the First Apocalypse of James contains a question-and-answer passage in which three toll collectors are said to demand toll and take souls by theft; James is instructed how to answer them.

Peer-reviewed scholarship describes this as James's postmortem journey through celestial toll collectors.

Disposition:

```text
TOLL_COLLECTOR_PASSAGE_IN_FIRST_APOCALYPSE_OF_JAMES = PROVEN
SCRIPTED_Q_AND_A = PROVEN
POSTMORTEM_ASCENT_CONTEXT = PROVEN
```

### E. Critical identity split: work vs surviving Greek fragments

The published Greek fragments P.Oxy. 5533 preserve selected portions of the work. Comparative scholarship maps the extant Greek leaves mainly against Coptic passages around NHC V,3.29–30 and 37–39.

The toll-collector dialogue is located in the Coptic witness around NHC V,3.33–34.

Therefore:

```text
FIRST_APOCALYPSE_OF_JAMES_CONTAINS_TOLL_PASSAGE = PROVEN
SURVIVING_P_OXY_5533_FRAGMENTS_CONTAIN_TOLL_PASSAGE = NOT SHOWN
WORK_IDENTITY != EXTANT_FRAGMENT_CONTENT
```

If the screenshot's phrase "this specific text" means the *work* First Apocalypse of James, it survives.  
If it means the *specific Greek fragments discovered in 2017 contain that toll-collector passage*, the edge does not close.

### F. "Dying person" language

Some scholarship interprets the ascent instructions as useful for a dying believer/martyr; the immediate narrative addresses James and his anticipated death.

Disposition:

```text
JAMES_POSTMORTEM_INSTRUCTIONS = PROVEN
GENERAL_DYING_PERSON_APPLICATION = SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION
```

### G. Modern "reincarnation trap" genealogy

The First Apocalypse of James clearly contains hostile/cosmic powers, postmortem ascent, toll collectors, and an escape-through-correct-response motif.

But the surviving text does not by itself establish the modern online doctrine usually called a "reincarnation trap." Older traditions also contain related but non-identical motifs:

- Egyptian Book of the Dead: spells intended to help the deceased navigate dangers in the afterlife.
- Orphic-Bacchic gold tablets: instructions/password-like guidance for the dead.
- Plato's Myth of Er: souls choose new lives in a reincarnation cycle.

These parallels do not prove direct ancestry either.

Disposition:

```text
ANCIENT_POSTMORTEM_GATEKEEPER_MOTIF = PROVEN
ANCIENT_REINCARNATION_MOTIFS = PROVEN
MODERN_REINCARNATION_TRAP = SAME_DOCTRINE = NOT PROVEN
FIRST_APOCALYPSE -> MODERN_REINCARNATION_TRAP_DIRECT_GENEALOGY = HOLD
"EARLIEST ORIGIN" = HOLD
```
