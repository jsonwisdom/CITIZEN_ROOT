JASON_QUAD_ONION_CLAIM_V0

CLAIM:

Jason’s Quad Onion is a layered reasoning architecture in which each layer may feed the next, but no layer proves the next.

ROOT INVARIANT:

EACH_LAYER_FEEDS_THE_NEXT
NONE_PROVE_THE_NEXT

CLAIMED LAYERS:

O1 — EXPERIENCE / APPLIED WISDOM
Experience may generate questions, observations, learning, and candidate evidence.

O2 — CONTINUITY / BLOSSOM
Objects, versions, historical state, current state, and counterfactual state remain distinguishable across time.

O3 — RECEIPT
Claims may be bound to observations, evidence, hashes, receipts, counter-receipts, replay procedures, and unresolved state.

O4 — AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
Observation, verification, authority, execution, receipt, and legal effect remain separate predicates.

CLAIMED FLOW:

APPLE_BLOSSOM
→ O1 EXPERIENCE
→ O2 CONTINUITY
→ O3 RECEIPT
→ O4 AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY
→ REPLAY

CLAIMED MEMBRANES:

EXPERIENCE != RECEIPT
MEMORY != CONTINUITY_RECEIPT
RECEIPT != AUTHORITY
VERIFICATION != AUTHORITY
AUTHORIZED != EXECUTED
EXECUTED != RECEIVED
RECEIVED != LEGAL_EFFECT
PERSISTENCE != PROMOTION
PROMOTION != AUTHORITY
SAME_NAME != SAME_BYTES
OBSERVED_DEFINITION != CANONICAL_SELECTION

CLAIMED REPLAY RULE:

A claim may be observed, challenged, held, replayed, corrected, and versioned without requiring deletion of prior observations or silent promotion of unresolved material.

CLAIMED FAILURE-CLOSED POSTURE:

STRUCTURAL_INVALIDITY → INVALID
REQUIRED_ASSERTION_FAILURE → FAILED
MISSING_OR_UNRESOLVED_INPUT → HOLD
ALL_REQUIRED_REPLAY_CHECKS_PASS → VERIFIED_RECEIPT

VERIFIED_RECEIPT != AUTHORITY

CLAIMED PURPOSE:

The architecture is intended to preserve provenance, continuity, challenge, replay, correction, and authority boundaries while allowing the same reasoning grammar to scale from individual and family learning objects to organizational and institutional records.
