## Claim split

### CLAIM_A — “The Federal Reserve has secretly been replaced.”
Disposition: REJECT_AS_STATED

Current Federal Reserve records continue to show the Federal Reserve System operating under its statutory structure: Board of Governors, FOMC, and 12 Reserve Banks. Current Board materials list Kevin Warsh as Chair and the Board remains active. A change in chairmanship is not replacement of the Federal Reserve as an institution.

Primary sources:
- Federal Reserve structure: https://www.federalreserve.gov/faqs/about_12593.htm
- Federal Reserve Board members: https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/bios/board/

### CLAIM_B — “The government is driving gold higher to reset/offset federal debt and liabilities.”
Disposition: HOLD / NO_RECEIPT

No controlling public record located in this replay establishes a federal program to raise gold prices for a debt reset. Treasury continues to report U.S. gold at the statutory accounting value of $42.2222 per fine troy ounce in its international reserve position. Federal Reserve research published September 3, 2026 attributes the recent rise in the market value of global gold reserves primarily to a surge in gold prices associated with private-sector demand, not a disclosed U.S. debt-reset operation.

Primary sources:
- Treasury U.S. International Reserve Position, July 31, 2026: https://home.treasury.gov/data/us-international-reserve-position/07312026
- Federal Reserve note on gold reserve comparisons, Sept. 3, 2026: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/why-gold-didnt-actually-overtake-treasury-securities-as-the-worlds-favorite-reserve-asset-20260903.html

### CLAIM_C — “Gold and silver will be used to buy BTC, LTC, ETH, XRP and stablecoins.”
Disposition: REJECT_AS_CURRENT_POLICY / FUTURE_INTENT_HOLD

The March 6, 2025 Executive Order created a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate U.S. Digital Asset Stockpile. It directs Treasury and Commerce to develop budget-neutral strategies for acquiring additional Government BTC. For non-BTC stockpile assets, the order states that the U.S. Government shall not acquire additional assets other than through forfeiture or civil penalties without further executive or legislative action. The order does not establish a gold/silver liquidation program to acquire BTC, LTC, ETH, XRP, or stablecoins.

Primary source:
- White House Executive Order, March 6, 2025: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishment-of-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-and-united-states-digital-asset-stockpile/

### CLAIM_D — “Tokenization/stablecoin policy is expanding.”

Federal policy does support expansion of regulated digital-asset activity, including tokenization, custody, and stablecoin issuance. The GENIUS Act became Public Law 119-27 on July 18, 2025. As of September 2026, multiple implementing rules remain in proposed-rulemaking stages. This policy direction does not prove CLAIM_A, CLAIM_B, or CLAIM_C.

## Exchange-custody claim

“Not your keys, not your coins” describes the practical distinction between self-custody and custodial exchange control, but the blanket statement “you do not own crypto on an exchange” is too broad. Customer legal rights depend on the exchange, account agreement, asset, and jurisdiction. Exchanges can restrict withdrawals under contractual, compliance, security, or lawful-government processes. Self-custody also introduces private-key loss, phishing, malware, and irreversible-transfer risks.

The Telegram call-to-action in the supplied post is not treated as evidence of any government or market claim.
